## 1. What Mills is

Mills is a coordination workspace for residential general contractors ("GCs") and the trades they work with. It helps a GC track job schedules, trade status and draft updates. During the pilot Mills runs in **mock mode**: it records what *would* be sent and does not send email, SMS or other messages to anyone.
