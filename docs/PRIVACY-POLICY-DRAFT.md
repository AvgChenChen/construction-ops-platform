## 1. Who this covers

- **Customers and their users** — GCs and their staff who use Mills.
- **People in Customer Data** — trades, contractors and other contacts that a Customer adds. For this data the Customer decides what is collected and why; Mills processes it on the Customer's behalf. **[LAWYER: controller/processor roles.]**
- **Visitors** to the Mills website.

## 2. What we collect

| Data | Source | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account data: name, business, email, sign-in identifiers | You | Provide accounts and sign-in |
| Job data: schedules, tasks, trade status, notes | You | Run the coordination workspace |
| Contacts: names, companies, roles, emails, phone numbers of trades | You | Show who is responsible for each task; draft updates |
| Uploaded documents (when uploads are enabled), which may include schedules, plans, or resumes | You | Extract schedule facts and draft updates for your review |
| Email content (only if you later connect a mailbox) | You, via an integration you enable | Find schedule updates you ask Mills to look for |
| Security logs: sign-in events, audit trail of changes, IP-derived rate-limit keys | Automatic | Security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting |

We do not collect payment card numbers during the pilot.

## 3. How we use it

To provide and secure the service, to support you, to keep an audit trail of changes, and to improve Mills using aggregated, de-identified information. We do not sell personal information and do not use Customer Data for advertising.

## 4. AI processing

When AI features are enabled, parts of your Customer Data may be sent to an AI model provider to produce drafts or summaries. Mills will (a) list those providers as subprocessors, (b) choose providers whose terms do not allow training on your data, and (c) always require a person on your team to review Output before anything is sent. AI features are **off** during the pilot. **[LAWYER/FOUNDERS: confirm provider terms before enabling.]**

Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and for backups, hashed session tokens, least-privilege access for trades, an audit log of changes, and redaction of contact details and credentials from application logs. No system is perfectly secure.
